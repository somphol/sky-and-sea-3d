Field guide · quiet instructions

A small ritual for deep attention.

01

Set the world

Choose Ocean Drift or SkyCraft Soar, then paint the light with one of four atmosphere studies. The camera keeps its own slow orbit — no input required.

02

Grow the sound

Awaken the sound garden, then balance surf, rain, chimes and a 432 Hz binaural tone. Opening the long-form stream library starts the last selected mix automatically; use Play stream if autoplay is blocked.

03

Begin a focus

A 25-minute focus block is followed by a five-minute break. The completion chime is soft; leave auto-break on to keep the rhythm.

04

Keep the record

Focus time, completed sessions and your preferred atmosphere are saved in this browser. Save a JSON study log to carry them to another device.
